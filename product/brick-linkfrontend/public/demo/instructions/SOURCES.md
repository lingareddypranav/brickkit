# Cached picture guide sources

Derived from the OMR models credited below; original credits and licences are also retained in each guide.mpd.
Step grouping for Green Dinosaur, Apatosaurus and Pteranodon was added by the Blueprint project. Space Transport and Shuttle Explorer each move one OMR step earlier so its pieces stay visible. Other guides retain the OMR construction order; some steps use a second camera angle to show pieces on the far side.
Part previews are rendered with the LDraw Parts Library. See [LDraw attribution and licences](../omr/CAreadme.txt), [CCAL](../omr/CAlicense.txt) and [CC BY 4.0](../omr/CAlicense4.txt).
